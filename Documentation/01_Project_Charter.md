# Project Overview

This project analyzes Amazon e-commerce sales data to uncover valuable business insights through SQL and Power BI.

The dataset consists of over 128,000 Amazon sales transactions containing information about orders, products, shipping, fulfillment methods, promotions, quantities, and sales amounts.

The objective is to transform raw business reports into a structured SQL database, perform exploratory and advanced SQL analysis, and develop interactive Power BI dashboards that support business decision-making.

This project demonstrates an end-to-end analytics workflow from raw CSV data to executive-level reporting.

---

# Business Problem

Large e-commerce businesses generate thousands of transactions every day.

Without structured reporting, it becomes difficult to answer important business questions such as:

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which categories perform best?
- Which locations generate the most sales?
- Which fulfillment methods are most effective?
- What percentage of orders are cancelled?
- How do sales change over time?

The goal of this project is to organize, analyze, and visualize sales data to support better business decisions.
